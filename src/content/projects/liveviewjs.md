Simple yet powerful framework for LiveViews in NodeJS and Deno

Phoenix LiveView is amazing but in Elixir (a language that I didn't know well).  LiveViewJS was born out of the question of could I use the same front-end library from Phoenix but use a different back-end language (namely JavaScript/TypeScript).  The answer was yes, and LiveViewJS is the result.
