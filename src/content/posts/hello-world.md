## Imposter syndrome

Part of the reason I've never consistently published content is that I've always had a bit of imposter syndrome and I am not really seeking to be an "influecer".  But as I've gotten older, I've become more confident that I have a unique view on the world and some ideas that I want to share them.

## Writing is thinking and new habits are hard

For me, writing has always helped me clarify my thinking.  We'll see if I can get comfortable putting thoughts out here that aren't perfectly polished.  New habits are hard...
